      Glossary
      Implied value per share
      The equity value per share this DCF produces on the stated assumptions: the present value of 20 years of projected net income, plus cash and short-term investments, less total debt, divided by diluted shares outstanding and adjusted for dilution. It is a model output, not a forecast, price target or recommendation.
      Current share price
      The latest traded price of one share.
      Revenue
      Total income from the sale of goods and services in a fiscal year (the top line).
      Net profit margin
      Net income as a percentage of revenue.
      CAGR
      Compound annual growth rate: the constant yearly rate that takes a figure from its starting value to its ending value over a period.
      Discount rate
      The annual rate used to convert future earnings to present value. At 0%, a dollar earned in year 20 counts the same as a dollar today.
      Dilution
      The reduction in existing shareholders' ownership when new shares are issued, often through stock-based compensation.
      SEC-verified
      Figures reconciled to the company's filings with the US Securities and Exchange Commission (10-K and 10-Q reports on EDGAR).

      13F portfolios of notable investors

      Updated automatically when new filings appear.

      Where this information comes from

      • SEC Form 13F filings on EDGAR. Every investment manager with more than $100 million in US-listed stocks must report its holdings within 45 days of each quarter end. Each investor has a link to their latest filing.
      • What 13F leaves out: short positions, cash, bonds, most non-US shares, private companies, and trades made during the quarter. So these portfolios can be up to four and a half months old and are not the investors' full positions.
      • Performance is an estimate (“13F replication”): each reported portfolio is held unchanged until the next quarter end, priced at the share prices implied by all 13F reports for that date. Dividends are not included. It is not the investors' actual fund return.
      • US stock market comparison: the same calculation applied to Vanguard's 13F holdings, which cover almost every US stock.
      • Concentrated means the 10 largest positions make up at least 60% of the reported portfolio.
      • Some investors don't file a 13F, for example Peter Thiel's Founders Fund and Nick Sleep, so they can't be tracked here.
